Space

A day on Venus is longer than its year

One of the strangest facts about our neighbouring planet.

Venus takes about 243 Earth days to rotate once on its axis, but only about 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun. Its rotation is exceptionally slow.

Nature

The octopus has three hearts

This ocean animal has a remarkable circulatory system.

Two hearts pump blood toward the gills, while a third pumps blood to the rest of the body. Octopus blood also contains a copper-based protein that helps carry oxygen.

Science

Lightning is incredibly hot

A flash of lightning can heat the air to extreme temperatures.

The air in a lightning channel can briefly reach around 30,000°C, roughly five times the temperature of the Sun's visible surface.
🏛️
History

Cleopatra lived closer to the Moon landing

Ancient history can be surprising when you look at the dates.

Cleopatra VII lived from 69 to 30 BCE. The Great Pyramid of Giza was already more than 2,000 years old in her lifetime, and the 1969 Moon landing was closer to her era than pyramid construction was.
🌳
Nature

Trees can communicate through ecosystems

Forests are more connected than they might appear.

Plants can exchange chemical signals and interact with fungi around their roots. Scientists study how these networks influence forest ecosystems; popular descriptions sometimes exaggerate what is known.
🧬
Science

Your body is constantly renewing itself

Many of your cells are replaced throughout your life.

Different cell types renew at different rates. The lining of your intestine turns over quickly, while many nerve cells can last for decades. There is no single timetable for the entire body.
