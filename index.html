Why Is Space So Dark?

With billions of stars in the universe, why does space look black?

Space looks dark because the universe is expanding and has a finite age. Light from many distant stars and galaxies has not had enough time to reach us. Light also spreads out as the universe expands. The night sky is not filled with visible light from every direction.

Nature

The Octopus Has Three Hearts

This ocean animal has a surprisingly unusual circulatory system.

An octopus has three hearts. Two pump blood toward the gills, where oxygen is collected. The third pumps blood to the rest of the body. Octopus blood is blue because it uses a copper-containing protein called hemocyanin to carry oxygen.

Science

Your Brain Is Always Working

Your brain keeps managing important processes even while you sleep.

Your brain helps control breathing, memory, movement, and many other functions. During sleep, it remains active and cycles through different stages. Sleep also supports learning and memory. Your brain does not simply switch off when you go to bed.

A Day Longer Than a Year

One planet has a rotation period longer than its trip around the Sun.

Venus takes about 243 Earth days to rotate once relative to the distant stars, but it takes about 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun. Venus also rotates in the opposite direction to most planets. Its thick atmosphere makes its surface extremely hot.

Nature

How Trees Communicate

Forests contain complex underground connections between living things.

Tree roots can connect with fungi that form networks in the soil. These fungi help plants obtain nutrients and water. Scientists study how resources and chemical signals move through these relationships. However, popular claims about trees deliberately talking like humans often oversimplify the science.

History

The Mystery of Ancient Pyramids

How did ancient builders move enormous blocks of stone?

Ancient Egyptian pyramid builders used skilled labour, planning, tools, and systems for transporting stone. Archaeological evidence helps researchers understand how construction worked. Some details remain debated, but there is strong evidence of human engineering, not a need to assume supernatural explanations.

Science

Lightning Is Extremely Hot

A lightning channel can become hotter than the surface of the Sun.

The air in a lightning channel can briefly reach temperatures around 30,000 kelvin, several times hotter than the Sun's visible surface. The rapid heating makes the air expand suddenly. That expansion produces the sound wave we hear as thunder.

The Deep Ocean's Hidden World

Much of the deep seafloor remains difficult to explore.

The deep ocean has crushing pressure, near-freezing temperatures in many areas, and almost complete darkness. Special submersibles and remotely operated vehicles help researchers study it. New observations continue to reveal unfamiliar animals and ecosystems, including communities around hydrothermal vents.

Space

What Are Shooting Stars?

The bright streaks in the night sky are not usually stars.

A shooting star is a meteor: a streak of light produced when a small piece of space debris enters Earth's atmosphere and heats the surrounding gas. Most meteors are caused by tiny particles. If a piece survives and reaches the ground, it is called a meteorite.

Why Do We Dream?

Dreams are a fascinating part of the sleeping brain.

Dreams can happen during several stages of sleep and are often vivid during REM sleep. Researchers have different ideas about why we dream. Possible explanations include processing emotions, organising memories, and the brain making sense of activity during sleep. There is no single explanation for every dream.
